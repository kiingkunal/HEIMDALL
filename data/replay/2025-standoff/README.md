# 2025 Standoff — Replay Dataset

Immutable historical window for HEIMDALL's backtest: **2025-05-01 → 2025-06-30**, the
June 2025 Israel–Iran escalation and Strait of Hormuz closure scare.

## Why this window

Brent ran from ~$63 (calm) to an intraday **$80.37 on 2025-06-19** on Hormuz-closure fears,
then collapsed to ~$69 after the **2025-06-24 ceasefire**. A clean, real, defensible
signal→spike→de-escalation arc that exercises every HEIMDALL engine on authentic data.

## Honesty status (Truth Constraint #4)

- **Prices are real.** Pulled from EIA, the authoritative source. Not synthetic.
- **Evidence is sourced and immutable.** Truth Constraint #4 permits a curated immutable evidence
  dataset when each event retains its original URL and timestamp. That is the implemented lane.
  It does not claim GDELT tone or GDELT bulk extraction. Qualitative event-type tone inputs are
  explicit authored rules in `config/replay_backtest.yaml`.
- **Chronological only.** `loader.py` streams events strictly in timestamp order; every read is
  checked by `leakage_guard.py` against the sim clock. Future-data leakage raises, and is tested for.
- **Thresholds are locked before evaluation.** `make replay-lock` hashes the risk, corridor,
  cascade, Monte Carlo, solver, and backtest configs. `make backtest` refuses any mismatch.
- **Metrics are bounded claims.** `docs/metrics.md` identifies the premium figure as a planning
  counterfactual and does not infer precision/false-alert rates from one event window.

## Do not

- Do not describe the curated evidence lane as GDELT-derived.
- Do not present the counterfactual premium as booked savings.
- Do not convert day-precision events or daily EIA prints into an exact intraday lead-time claim.
